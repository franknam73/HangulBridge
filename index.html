Target Weaknesses Handled in Every Lesson:
  • Clause Complexification: Transforming basic subject-object-verb clauses into connected thoughts with proper conjunctions (~(으)면서, ~(으)ㄴ데, ~(으)므로).
  • Particle Accuracy: Precision between subjective 이/가, topic 은/는, dynamic location 에서 vs static 에, and tool/direction (으)로.
  • Tone Switching: Seamless transitions from informal polite (해요체) to formal business Korean (하십시오체) and subtle honorific markers (시).
